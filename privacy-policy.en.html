Privacy Policy for 3 Seconds

Effective date: 26 October 2026
Developer: KTL Games
Privacy contact: kutlaygames@gmail.com

This policy explains how 3 Seconds handles information when you play the Android game. The developer named above is responsible for the app. Please contact us at the privacy contact above with questions or requests.

Information kept on your device

The game saves high and recent scores, coins, challenge progress and history, language choice, sound and music settings, vibration, and purchase entitlement status on your device. These records are used to run the game and remember your preferences. The game does not use its own account or server to store your scores. You can remove local app data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app; device backup or restore settings may affect copies.

Advertising and consent

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The currently planned normal release does not request live ads through game code; internal test versions can request Google sample ads. Included SDK components may still process technical information, so the absence of a visible ad does not itself mean that no data is transmitted. When these components operate, Google may receive an IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), app interactions, diagnostics and performance information, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID or App Set ID. This information may be used for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. UMP checks whether a privacy message is required, records applicable choices, and may communicate with Google. Where required, choices can be reviewed through the in-app privacy options entry point when it is available. Google's processing is described in its own privacy information.

Purchases

The app includes Unity In-App Purchasing and Google Play Billing for a Remove Ads entitlement. Starting a new purchase is disabled in the currently planned normal release, but the app can still contact Google Play to check or restore an existing entitlement. The game can receive product, receipt, and ownership information and stores an entitlement indicator locally. Google Play handles payment details; the game code does not ask you for a card number. Unity IAP may process short-lived technical diagnostics needed for its service. Google Play and Unity may handle related information under their own policies.

Sharing a score

If you tap Share, the game creates a score-card image and message in local temporary storage and opens Android's share sheet. You choose the receiving app and recipient. The game gives that app temporary access to the image; it does not upload the score card to a developer-operated server. The receiving app's handling of the content is governed by its own policy. Temporary cards may remain in app cache until Android or you clear it.

Retention, security, and your choices

Local game records remain until you clear app data or uninstall, subject to device backup settings. Third-party providers set their own retention periods. We do not state a single deletion period for data held by Google or Unity. You can control optional score sharing, Android advertising-ID settings, and any available in-app privacy choices. Contact the privacy address above to ask about information handled by the developer; provider-held data may also require a request to the provider. We use platform and provider security measures, but no transmission or storage method is guaranteed risk-free.

Children and changes

We do not knowingly ask players for names, contact details, or precise location in the game. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, contact us. We will update this page when material practices or SDK configurations change, with a new effective date.
